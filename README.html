RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA protocol

A language model and a 3D folding program, from different papers, joined by one hand-off. The only input is the RNA sequence: RibonanzaNet2 reads it and proposes base pairs, including pseudoknots, and SimRNA folds a 3D chain under those pairs. The crystal structure is never an input. It is used only after the prediction, to score it, and in a separate validation experiment (section 3) that compared RibonanzaNet2's pairs with other sources.

1 Targets

The three exercise riboswitches

The three targets of the exercise, all part of RNAnneal's 16-riboswitch benchmark. Each has at least one pseudoknot, the case EternaFold-type tools cannot predict. Pair counts are canonical base pairs (AU, GC, GU) annotated from the crystal with rnapolis, numbered on the modelled chain; RNAnneal's Table I lists slightly different constructs and counts (53 nt for 6TFF, 90 nt for 6Q57, 5 pseudoknot pairs for 6WJR).

2A · Language model: which letters pair?

Sequence only

The A/C/G/U string. No alignment of relatives, no template, no structure.

RibonanzaNet2, frozen

48 layers, 12 attention heads each, plus a running table of notes on every pair of letters. Trained on chemical-probing data from millions of RNAs, not on 3D structures.

Read one attention head: layer 25, head 1

  • Chosen among 576 heads on 612 training RNAs
  • Not tuned on the three targets, but the 612 include 4LVW, a 99% copy of 6Q57; it influenced only which of the 576 heads was read
  • Close to IPknot and EternaFold in pair accuracy on 185 test RNAs (mean F1 within 0.02), and finds about 10× more pseudoknot pairs on 60 structures released after April 2025

Decode pairs (rules fixed before scoring)

  • |i − j| ≥ 4; AU / GC / GU only
  • Greedy, one partner per letter, round(0.3 L) pairs
  • Crossing pairs are kept: those are the pseudoknots
2B · SimRNA: fold a 3D chain under those pairs

Hand-off: pairs as dot-bracket layers

SimRNA takes one line of brackets per layer of pairs that don't cross. The pseudoknot goes on its own line. 6Q57, RibonanzaNet2 arm:

GGAGAGUAGAUGAUUCGCGUUAAGUGUGUGUGAAUGGGAUGUCGUCACACAACGAAGCGAGAGCGCGGUGAAUCAUUGCAUCCGCUCCA ((((.....((((((((((....(...((((((.(........)))))))..)..........)))...)))))))........)))). ....................................(((((.....................................)))))......

Line 2 is the pseudoknot: all 5 of its crystal pairs are found by RibonanzaNet2, none by EternaFold.

Replica-exchange Monte Carlo folding

  • Each nucleotide is 5 beads; energy from statistics of known RNA structures
  • Given pairs are added as attractive restraints
  • 10 copies at temperatures 1.35 → 0.90, swapping as they go
  • 8 independent runs × 10 copies × 1.6 M steps (10% of the SimRNA benchmark length)
  • 10–40 min per run on 10 CPU cores, depending on length; all 96 runs (3 RNAs × 4 sources of pairs × 8) in parallel

Pick the answer by clustering

  • Pool all 32,080 snapshots per RNA and arm
  • Keep the lowest-energy 1%, group them at 0.1 × L Å RMSD
  • Centre of the largest group = the prediction; groups 2–5 kept as alternatives
  • Rebuild all atoms from the beads

Score, after the prediction (optional)

Needs an experimental structure of the same RNA, which plays no part in building the model.

  • TM-score and C1′ RMSD (US-align; TM > 0.45 ≈ right fold)
  • INF: agreement between the model's and the crystal's base pairs, √(precision × recall), 0–1
  • Pseudoknot pairs formed
  • RMSD and INF are the measures RNAnneal reports, so the two can be compared
3 Validation experiment, not part of the protocol

Was RibonanzaNet2 the right source of pairs?

To justify the protocol, SimRNA was also run with three other sources of pairs and the results compared. Only the pairs change between arms, so any difference in the 3D result comes from the pairs. EternaFold stands in for the pairing step RNAnneal uses; it cannot produce pseudoknots. The crystal-pairs arm is a ceiling control: it shows the best SimRNA can do with perfect pairs, and is the only place the crystal was ever fed to SimRNA. The protocol itself uses only the rnet2 arm.

4 What it tests
Does the language model help 3D?rnet2 vs eterna: same folding, better pairs and pseudoknots. A gain here is the language model's contribution.
How much is left?rnet2 vs xtal: the gap is the cost of the language model's wrong or missing pairs. xtal vs crystal: the limit of SimRNA itself.
How does it compare with RNAnneal?RNAnneal benchmarked the same three riboswitches with EternaFold-type pairs and FARFAR2. Section 7 sets its published numbers next to ours.

The language model's pairs get throughWith RibonanzaNet2 pairs SimRNA forms more real pairs than with EternaFold (INF 0.72 vs 0.67) and scores a slightly higher TM (0.28 vs 0.26), though not a lower RMSD (16.4 vs 15.2 Å). It is the only predicted source that builds 6Q57's pseudoknot, 5 of 5 pairs.
The fold is still wrongEvery model is below TM 0.45. Even the crystal's own pairs average 12 Å off, and only the 52-nt 6TFF folds well with them (2.8 Å). Helices form but are packed wrongly: the limit is SimRNA's 3D arrangement, not the pairs.
A short searchRuns were 10% of SimRNA's published benchmark length, and TM-score is harsh on RNAs of 50–110 nt. Longer runs may help a little, but section 6 shows the near-crystal shapes were mostly never reached.

BEDROC (0–1) measures how strongly a score puts near-crystal candidates first. The rank correlation for RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA is a different measure; both say whether the energy tracks correctness, and SimRNA's hardly does. The preprint's text says 6WJR had a representative within 5 Å, but its Fig. 2e shows no candidate closer than about 15 Å, so 6WJR's top-ranked entry is left open. RNAnneal's RMSD is likely over all atoms; RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA is scored over one backbone atom per nucleotide.

Same picture on 6TFFBoth pipelines put a wrong shape first (14.0 vs 13.8 Å) and both produce a near-crystal shape only as a minor alternative (2.3 vs 3.1 Å). RNAnneal's is a ranked state; for RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA it is a single snapshot, and the best model is at 6.4 Å.
RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA reaches a little closerOn 6WJR and 6Q57 the closest shape RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA produces is 13.6 and 6.5 Å, against about 15 and 7.5 Å for RNAnneal, and only RibonanzaNet2 + SimRNA forms 6Q57's pseudoknot. That is the RibonanzaNet2 pairs at work, at a fraction of the compute.
RNAnneal chooses far betterIts score ranks near-crystal shapes first much more strongly than Rosetta or the force field, while SimRNA's energy barely tracks correctness. The natural combination: RibonanzaNet2 pairs to generate candidates, RNAnneal's score to pick among them.
Inside the 3D model

How SimRNA turns a sequence and its pairs into coordinates

SimRNA has no neural network. It is a physics-style simulation: a simplified chain is moved at random, and each move is kept or rejected by an energy score. The language model's pairs enter only as extra energy rewards.

Use it

Run it on any sequence

The protocol is packaged as one command that, like RNAnneal, needs only the sequence. It does not include the EternaFold, no-pairs and crystal-pairs arms; those were comparisons to show that the RibonanzaNet2 pairs are worth using. A reference structure is optional and is used only to score the result, never to make it.

# sequence only: models and base pairs bash scripts/run_slurm.sh myrna GGAGAGUAGAUGAUUCGCGUUAAGUGUGUGUGAAUGG... # with the experimental structure: also TM-score, RMSD and INF bash scripts/run_slurm.sh 6Q57 GGAGAGUAGAUGAUUCG... data/structures/6Q57.cif A # without Slurm, on one machine bash scripts/run_local.sh myrna myrna.fasta

TM-score0 to 1, normalised by the reference. Above 0.45 means the same overall fold.
RMSDC1′ distance in Å over the whole chain, residue by residue. The core RMSD covers only the part US-align could superpose, so it always looks better.
INFAgreement between the model's and the reference's canonical base pairs, √(precision × recall), 0 to 1 (rnapolis annotation).

Without a reference no accuracy score is possible; the report gives cluster sizes instead. A large first cluster means the runs agree, not that the model is right. Code: scripts/run_slurm.sh, scripts/run_local.sh and the rnet2_simrna/ package.
